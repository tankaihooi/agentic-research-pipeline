## Stripe Credit Card Fees (Online & In-Person)

Stripe’s transaction fees vary depending on the type of payment being processed. Debit card transactions typically incur lower fees compared to credit card transactions. This section will provide a detailed breakdown of these fees, including gateway fees, highlighting the costs associated with online payments, in-person payments via Stripe Terminal, and ACH and wire transfers.

Grasping these distinctions helps businesses accurately anticipate payment processing expenses and make informed decisions about which payment methods to offer customers.

### Online (Card-Not-Present) Payments

For online payments, Stripe’s standard transaction fee structure is 2.9% plus a fixed fee of $0.30 per transaction. This applies to most credit card transactions, including those made through digital wallets like Apple Pay and Google Pay. Debit cards are also accepted for online payments and typically incur lower fees compared to credit cards.

However, these transaction fees can vary by location and whether the payment is domestic or international. Businesses should be aware of these potential variations to accurately calculate their costs when accepting payments from international customers.

### In-Person (Stripe Terminal) Payments

In-person payments processed through Stripe Terminal have a slightly different fee structure. The standard fee for these transactions is 2.7% plus a fixed fee of $0.05 per transaction. Debit cards are accepted for in-person payments and typically incur lower fees compared to credit cards. This makes Stripe Terminal a competitive option for businesses that accept card payments in a physical setting.

The cost of the card readers required for Stripe Terminal transactions ranges from $59 to $249, and fees can vary based on whether the card is from the EEA or non-EEA regions.

### Stripe ACH, Wire, and Bank Transfer Fees

For businesses that prefer ACH and wire transfers, Stripe offers a different fee structure, including specific ACH fees. Wire payments incur a fee of $8 per transaction. Meanwhile, ACH direct debit payments are charged at a rate of 0.8% with a maximum fee of $5, and ACH credit payments incur a flat fee of $1 per transaction. ACH returns (failed debits) cost $4 each.

These options can be particularly cost-effective for businesses dealing with large transaction amounts, as the lower processing fees are capped, limiting the maximum cost per transaction.

### Currency Conversion (FX) Fees

Currency conversion fees and foreign transaction fees are important considerations for businesses engaging in international transactions. Stripe applies a currency conversion fee of 1% on transactions that require converting one currency to another. This fee is added to the transaction amount and helps cover the costs associated with currency exchange.

For instance, if a business based in the United States processes a payment in euros, Stripe will convert the euros to US dollars and apply a 1% currency conversion fee to the transaction amount. However, if the business has a bank account denominated in euros, this fee is waived, as no currency conversion is necessary.

Understanding these fees is crucial for businesses that frequently deal with international customers. By being aware of the currency conversion fees, businesses can better anticipate their costs and manage their pricing strategies accordingly.

## Other Stripe Products & Fees (Billing, Connect, Radar, Sigma)

Beyond standard payment processing, Stripe offers a variety of additional services with associated monthly fees. These include virtual and physical card issuance, Stripe Terminal for in-person payments, and LLC formation solutions.

Stripe also offers Stripe Sigma, which provides detailed analytics and insights. The infrastructure fee for Stripe Sigma ranges from $10 to $100, depending on the business’s needs, with custom pricing available for businesses with over 50,000 charges per month.

### Stripe Billing

Stripe Billing is a versatile tool designed to help businesses manage invoices and subscription fees. It offers flexible pricing models, including subscription and usage-based billing, making it easier for businesses to handle recurring payments. This service is especially beneficial for businesses with complex billing needs, providing a streamlined solution for managing customer payments and subscriptions.

### Stripe Connect

Stripe Connect is designed to facilitate marketplace transactions, including platform fees, offering three key pricing models tailored to different needs. This service helps businesses manage payments efficiently, ensuring a smooth transaction process for both buyers and sellers.

With Stripe Connect, businesses can leverage Stripe’s robust platform to handle complex payment flows and manage payouts to multiple parties.

### Stripe Radar

Stripe Radar is a powerful fraud protection service that includes fraud detection fees and uses machine learning to detect and prevent fraudulent transactions. Its adaptive system continuously learns from new fraud patterns, enhancing its protective measures over time.

Using Stripe Radar helps businesses reduce potential losses from fraud, providing a more secure payment experience for customers.

## No Monthly Fees: But Watch These Other Costs

One of Stripe’s most appealing features is its lack of setup fees and monthly fees for standard payment processing services. This makes it an attractive option for businesses of all sizes, particularly those just starting out or with variable transaction volumes. However, there are other costs that businesses should be aware of:

* **Transaction Fees**: Stripe charges 2.9% + $0.30 per transaction for online payments. This fee structure is straightforward and easy to understand, making it simple for businesses to calculate their costs.
* **ACH Fees**: For ACH direct debit transactions, Stripe charges 0.8% of the transaction amount, capped at $5. ACH credit payments incur a flat fee of $1 per transaction.
* **Wire Fees**: Wire payments are subject to a fee of $8 per transaction, which can be a cost-effective option for large payments.
* **Refund Fees**: Issuing a refund incurs a fee of $0.30 per refund. This fee is applied to cover the costs associated with processing the refund.
* **Dispute Fees**: In the event of a payment dispute, Stripe charges a fee of $15 per dispute. This fee helps cover the costs of managing and resolving disputes.
* **Currency Conversion Fees**: As mentioned earlier, Stripe charges a 1% fee on international transactions that require currency conversion.

For businesses processing over $100,000 per month, Stripe offers custom pricing options. These can include lower processing fees, but may come with conditions such as minimum commitments. Custom pricing can be particularly beneficial for high-volume businesses, providing potential savings and more tailored fee structures.

Overall, while Stripe’s pricing is competitive with other payment processors, it’s important for businesses to carefully review all associated costs. By understanding the full scope of Stripe’s fees, businesses can make informed decisions and optimize their payment processing strategies.
