### Accelerated Checkout (Link)

Link is Stripe’s saved-payment-details product, and it’s the most meaningful addition to the platform since our last review. When a returning customer recognizes Link at checkout, their saved payment and address information autofill, which cuts the number of fields they have to complete.

Stripe says Link reaches more than 300 million consumers worldwide and helps businesses speed up checkout by up to three times for returning customers. The company also reports that vendors that enjoy a lot of repeat business see an average returning-user conversion increase of 14 percent.

We like that Link is included with Stripe’s standard payment processing tools rather than sold as a separate add-on. For subscription businesses, online retailers and other companies that rely on repeat purchases, it’s an easy way to streamline checkout without requiring customers to reenter their payment information every time they make a purchase.

Customers can save multiple payment methods with Link for faster checkout across Stripe-powered websites. Source: Stripe

### Subscription and Recurring Billing (Stripe Billing)

Stripe Billing helps businesses automate recurring payments and subscription billing while supporting a variety of pricing models, including:

* **Flexible pricing options:** Charge customers a flat recurring fee, bill by seat, create tiered pricing or charge based on product usage.
* **Custom billing schedules:** Set billing frequencies, offer discounts and free trials, and schedule future subscription changes.
* **Usage-based billing:** Track customer usage and automatically bill for metered services through Stripe’s integrated Metronome technology.

Stripe has simplified Billing pricing since our last review. Instead of choosing between Starter and Scale plans, businesses now pay 0.7 percent of billing volume. Basic usage-based billing features are included, while companies with more complex pricing models can use Metronome to support metered or hybrid billing.

Tip

Businesses with a subscription business model can use Stripe Billing to automate recurring payments, trial periods and usage-based pricing, making it easier to scale without manually tracking customer billing cycles.

Customers can use Stripe Billing’s self-service portal to manage subscriptions, update payment methods and view past billing activity. Source: Stripe.

### Marketplace and Platform Solutions (Stripe Connect)

Stripe Connect helps marketplaces and software platforms accept payments, onboard users and move money between multiple parties. Stripe handles many of the behind-the-scenes tasks, including identity verification, compliance requirements, tax reporting and payout management, making it easier to build and scale a multi-sided platform.

Businesses can use Connect to accept payments online, in person and through recurring billing models while automatically routing funds to sellers, contractors or service providers. We like that Stripe bundles these capabilities into a single platform instead of requiring separate providers for payment processing, onboarding and payouts.

Stripe Connect helps marketplaces and software platforms accept payments, onboard sellers and service providers, and manage payouts from a single platform. Source: Stripe

## Stripe Pricing

Stripe uses straightforward flat-rate pricing with no setup fees, monthly fees or long-term contracts. We appreciate that the company publishes its rates, surcharges and add-on pricing online instead of requiring a sales call.

Here’s a look at Stripe’s standard pricing.

| Transaction Type | Pricing  Key Details |
| --- | --- |
| Online card payments | 2.9% + $0.30 | Domestic cards and digital wallets |
| In-person payments | 2.7% + $0.05 | Stripe Terminal transactions |
| ACH Direct Debit | 0.8% (max $5) | Lower-cost option for bank transfers |
| Stablecoin payments | 1.5% | Includes conversion to fiat currency |

### Optional Add-Ons

Many of Stripe’s advanced products carry separate fees:

* **Stripe Billing:** 0.7 percent of billing volume on the pay-as-you-go plan
* **Stripe Connect:** Included with Payments, with additional fees depending on platform configuration
* **Radar for Fraud Teams:** Starts at $0.05 per screened transaction
* **Stripe Sigma:** $15 per month
* **Stripe Atlas:** $500 one-time fee
* **Stripe Tax:** 0.5 percent per transaction where you’re registered to collect tax

### Additional Pricing Considerations

There are a few other costs and policies to keep in mind when considering Stripe:

* **No monthly fees:** Stripe doesn’t charge monthly account fees, setup fees, PCI compliance fees or cancellation fees, which helps keep costs predictable.
* **International transactions cost more:** Transactions made with international cards add 1.5 percent to the standard processing rate. If currency conversion is required, Stripe charges an additional 1 percent.
* **Keyed transactions carry a surcharge:** Manually entered card payments add 0.5 percent to Stripe’s standard online processing rate.
* **Disputes can get expensive:** Stripe charges a $15 dispute-received fee for every dispute. If you manually contest a dispute, you’ll also pay a $15 dispute-countered fee, although that fee is refunded if you win.
* **Refunds aren’t completely free:** Stripe doesn’t charge a separate refund fee, but it doesn’t return the original processing fee when you issue a refund.
