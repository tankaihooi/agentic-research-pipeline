# August 2025 Subscription Pricing Updates for Revenue Recognition

**August 2025 pricing updates for Revenue Recognition**

Starting August 20, 2025, you can transition from pay-as-you-go pricing to subscription-based pricing for Revenue Recognition, our accrual accounting automation product. You can compare pricing plans in your Dashboard.

If you haven’t switched to a subscription plan by August 20, 2026, we’ll automatically turn off the product for your account. You can restore access at any time by subscribing.

**About Revenue Recognition**

Revenue Recognition turns complex accrual accounting calculations into automated reports. With Revenue Recognition, you can close your books same-day, accurately, and in line with IFRS 15 and ASC 606 standards directly from the Stripe Dashboard.

As a business using Revenue Recognition, you get access to latest product improvements that help you:

* Automate your accounting close in the same day, with data refreshed every three hours
* Save time with automated revenue calculations for subscriptions and usage-based billing events
* Improve accounting accuracy with journal entries that now reflect Stripe fees and prepaid payment credit notes

## **FAQs**

### **Why is Stripe Revenue Recognition transitioning to subscription-based pricing?**

Stripe Revenue Recognition is transitioning to subscription-based pricing in response to customer feedback, and to offer businesses more cost predictability.

### **How can I calculate my fees?**

### **Which subscription plan best fits my business?**

The right plan depends on your total Stripe Billing plus standalone payments volume. You’ll see a recommended plan based on your past payment volume in the Dashboard. You can upgrade (or downgrade) at any time without affecting the length of your current subscription term.

### **How does this affect my connected accounts?**

* *If you use Stripe Connect, your connected accounts use the Stripe Dashboard, and Stripe manages risk:* These changes will apply to the Stripe fees for your connected accounts. Stripe will automatically notify your connected accounts about the changes that affect them. There’s no action needed from you.
* *For all other businesses that use Stripe Connect:* You are responsible for any updates to your own pricing in line with these changes, and for communicating with your users

### **Which of my connected accounts are impacted?**

To understand which of your connected accounts are affected by this change, contact the Stripe Support team.

### **Why did I get this notice?**

This support page provides you with more details on the notice we sent you describing changes to certain fees. That notice is a legal notice sent to businesses that use Stripe, even those who have unsubscribed from optional marketing notices. You cannot unsubscribe from legal notices, but if you’d prefer not to receive any further legal notices from Stripe, you can close your account.

Your continued use of Stripe services after August 19, 2026, is subject to these fee changes. Any termination rights you have under your agreement with us are unaffected by this change.

### **Do I need to take action?**

Yes, you need to select a subscription plan by August 19, 2026. If you haven’t switched to a subscription plan by August 20, 2026, we’ll automatically turn off the product for your account.
