## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Stripe Billing cheaper than Zuora?

Generally, yes. Stripe uses a percentage-of-volume model that is transparent for smaller scales, whereas Zuora typically involves high platform fees and implementation costs.

### Can I use Stripe for payments and Zuora for billing?

Yes, this is a common enterprise configuration. Zuora acts as the orchestration and revenue engine, while Stripe serves as the underlying payment gateway.
