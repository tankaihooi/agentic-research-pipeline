## What is usage-based pricing?

Usage-based pricing is a billing approach in which charges correspond directly to a customer’s consumption of a product or service. Unlike flat rates or subscription billing, this model hinges on specific metrics, such as data usage volume, hours of service access, or quantity of items consumed. The result is a granular pricing system that adapts to each customer’s needs.

## FAQs about usage-based pricing

### What is the best usage-based pricing type?

There is no single best type, as the optimal model depends entirely on your product architecture, cost drivers, and target audience. Infrastructure platforms and API providers often thrive on pure pay-as-you-go unit pricing because their operational costs scale directly with compute or storage consumption.

For most modern SaaS platforms, a hybrid model combining a recurring base fee with variable usage overages offers the best outcome. This structure provides steady baseline revenue for your business while allowing pricing to scale naturally alongside customer adoption.

### How will usage based pricing impact my revenue predictability?

Pure usage-based pricing naturally increases revenue volatility compared to fixed recurring subscriptions because monthly income fluctuates with customer activity. Seasonal dips, macroeconomic shifts, or customer product changes can lead to unexpected contractions in billed consumption.
