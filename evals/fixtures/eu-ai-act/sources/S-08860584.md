# The EU AI Act timeline

Follow the regulation from publication through its phased application. Dates reflect the current framework, including the AI Omnibus changes in force from July 2026.

Applied Current milestone Upcoming

1. 1

   12 July 2024

   ## Published in the Official Journal

   Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 was published, beginning the countdown to entry into force.
2. 2

   1 August 2024

   ## The AI Act entered into force

   The regulation became EU law, with its obligations scheduled to apply in phases.
3. 3

   2 February 2025

   ## First rules began to apply

   The AI-system definition and prohibited-practice framework entered into application.
4. 4

   2 August 2025

   ## Governance and GPAI obligations

   EU and national governance provisions and obligations for general-purpose AI models began to apply.
5. 5

   27 July 2026

   ## AI Omnibus entered into force

   The amending regulation extended the high-risk implementation schedule and introduced targeted simplifications.
6. 6

   2 August 2026

   ## General application

   Most remaining provisions, including the Article 50 transparency framework, apply from this date.
7. 7

   2 December 2026

   ## Two new prohibitions and the legacy marking deadline

   Article 5(1)(ba) and (bb), inserted by the AI Omnibus, prohibit specified non-consensual intimate imagery and child sexual abuse material: taking the Article 5 list to ten. Generative systems already on the market must also carry machine-readable marking of synthetic output by this date.
8. 8

   2 August 2027

   ## Existing GPAI model transition ends

   Providers of GPAI models placed on the market before 2 August 2025 must meet the applicable obligations.
9. 9

   2 December 2027

   ## Annex III high-risk systems

   High-risk requirements begin applying to listed sensitive uses such as employment, education, essential services and biometrics.
10. 10

    2 August 2028

    ## AI embedded in regulated products

    High-risk requirements apply to AI systems integrated into products such as machinery, toys and lifts.
11. 11

    2 August 2030

    ## Legacy large-scale IT systems must comply

    AI components of Union large-scale IT systems in the area of freedom, security and justice, placed on the market before 2 August 2027, must be brought into compliance.

Source: Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 and European Commission AI Act implementation updates. Always verify dates against the current official text before legal reliance.
